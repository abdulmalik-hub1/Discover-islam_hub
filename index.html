Start with a question

You do not need to know anything about Islam before you begin.

Who is Allah?

“Allah” is the Arabic word for God. Islam teaches that God is One, without partners, and the Creator of all things.

Who is Muhammad ﷺ?

Muhammad ﷺ is regarded by Muslims as the final prophet and messenger of God, who conveyed the Qur’an and taught its principles through his example.

What is the Qur’an?

The Qur’an is the central scripture of Islam, believed by Muslims to be the word of God revealed to Muhammad ﷺ.

Why do Muslims pray?

Muslims pray to worship and remember God, seek guidance, express gratitude, and maintain a regular connection with Him.

What Islam means

One God. One human family. A life of purpose.

Islam means submission to God. Muslims believe in one God, His prophets, His revealed scriptures, the angels, the Day of Judgment, and divine decree.

A foundation

  • Shahadah — testimony of faith
  • Salah — five daily prayers
  • Zakah — obligatory charity
  • Sawm — fasting in Ramadan
  • Hajj — pilgrimage to Makkah when able

Learn responsibly

Good questions deserve good sources.

Each Qur’an verse, hadith, and historical claim should be connected to a clearly identified primary or reputable scholarly source.

Qur’an

Show the surah and verse number and identify the translation. Translations convey meaning rather than replacing the Arabic text.

Hadith

Identify the collection and reference, and distinguish authentic reports from disputed or weak narrations.

History

Separate religious belief from historical claims and provide sources when discussing people, dates, and events.

Ask a scholar

For detailed religious rulings or personal guidance, consult a qualified and trusted Islamic scholar.

Six Articles of Faith

Six Pillars of Faith.

Muslims believe in six essential matters of faith: Allah, His angels, His revealed books, His messengers, the Last Day, and divine decree.

Six Articles of Faith

Six essential matters of belief.

01

Belief in Allah

To believe that Allah is One, without partner, and the Creator and Lord of all things.

02

Belief in the Angels

To believe in the angels Allah created and in the roles He has assigned to them.

03

Belief in the Books

To believe in Allah’s revealed scriptures, including the Qur’an as His final revealed book.

04

Belief in the Messengers

To believe in Allah’s prophets and messengers, including Muhammad ﷺ as the final messenger.

05

Belief in the Last Day

To believe that people will be resurrected, judged, and held accountable for their deeds.

06

Belief in Divine Decree

To believe in Allah’s knowledge and decree, while people remain responsible for their choices and actions.
